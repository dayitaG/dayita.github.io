The Verdict
Housing: Dublin renters at 2.4× the national burden rate — highest in Ireland.
Commute: 78,000 Dubliners endure 45–60 min daily gridlock. Over half drive, flooding already-overloaded roads.
Rents: Up 87% since 2015 — double the national pace. At €2,315/mo, rent consumes 83% of avg take-home pay.
Composite: Dublin renters score 8.4/10 — the most stressed cohort in the country, globally ranked #2.
"83 cents of every euro earned goes straight to rent."
At an average net salary of €2,775 and average Dublin rent of €2,315, the arithmetic is brutal. No European capital of comparable size comes close.
